Here are three short options depending on how well you know the classmate:

### Option 1: Casual & Direct (For a friend)

> "Hey [Name], I missed class on [Day/Date]. Do you mind sharing your notes from that lecture when you get a chance? Thanks a lot!"

### Option 2: Friendly & Polite (For an acquaintance or classmate)

> "Hi [Name], I couldn’t make it to [Class Name] on [Day/Date]. Would you be open to sharing your notes with me? Happy to send you mine next time you need them!"

### Option 3: Formal (For a study group or class group chat)

> "Hi everyone, I was unable to attend [Class Name] on [Day/Date]. If anyone is willing to share their notes from that session, I’d really appreciate it. Thanks!"

---

**Tips to get a quick response:**

* Mention the exact **date** and **course name**.
* Offer to exchange notes in the future to keep it a two-way street.
