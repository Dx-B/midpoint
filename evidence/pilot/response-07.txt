Here are a few templates you can use to ask a classmate or friend for class notes, depending on the situation:

### 1. Casual / Friend (If you missed a class)

> "Hey [Name], I couldn't make it to [Class Name] on [Date]. Would you mind sharing your notes with me if you have them handy? I'd really appreciate it! Let me know if I can return the favor sometime."

### 2. Formal / Classmate (If you don't know them well)

> "Hi [Name], I noticed you're in my [Class Name] section. I missed class on [Date] due to [brief reason, e.g., an appointment/feeling under the weather], and I'm trying to catch up. Would you be willing to share a photo or copy of your notes from that day? Thanks so much for your help!"

### 3. Study Group / General Class Chat

> "Hi everyone! Does anyone happen to have detailed notes from [Class Name] on [Date] that they’d be willing to share? I want to make sure I didn't miss anything key before the upcoming test. Thanks in advance!"

**Tips:**

* **Be specific:** Include the exact date and subject/topic so they know what to send.
* **Offer help in return:** Showing you're willing to share notes in the future makes people much more likely to help.
